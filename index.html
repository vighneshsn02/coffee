Direct Trade & Ethics

From High Altitude Soil To Your Cup

Founded in 2021, AURA was born out of an obsessive passion for single-origin traceability. We partner directly with organic smallholder farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Sumatra to ensure 100% fair living wages and sustainable farming practices.
